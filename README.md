# Nerds-unbridled

## Welcome to Nerds _Unbridled_!

We are an independent, student-run publication for essays that take a position and defend it. We publish work centered on politics and public life, but we're open to any subject a writer can make a real argument about—culture, science, ethics, and beyond. We hold the view that all things worldly are political, all things relate to each other, and all things must be deconstructed.

We are not affiliated with any single class, department, or student organization. Anyone—student or not—is welcome to submit.

The publishers of this paper take an explicitly left-wing approach to society and politics. Therefore, we encourage any pieces that develop on this theme. The point, however, is not to create a dogmatic journal, but to open a space for any work students may have done on politics, economy, society, culture, etc. that, rather than depreciating in the bowels of our own insecurities as students, may be granted the space to live out there on the vast interwebs, granted a trial in the court of public opinion, allowing your voice to reach the world.

We thus welcome many genres, and will take many efforts to provide adequate space for varying themes, clearly delineated so.

## On the code

The code for this website was mostly written by me. The original backend, however, was written using the assistance of ClaudeAI (because I really don't know javascript, and just wanted something that worked---and it did). 

It will be subject to continuous improvements, in the hopes that I can improve my own skill in the process and slowly allow this project to operate with a more _personal_ touch.

Cheers to anyone following along on this project, and please feel free to suggest improvements, I would be eternally grateful.
